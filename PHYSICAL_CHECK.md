## Blind diagram check

1. Give the reviewer the PNG and the corresponding question from the task manifest. Shuffle the order and keep the reference, reveal, and model replies aside. Use both signature tasks and a mix of all three evaluation families.
2. For each task, record its ID, the reviewer’s answer, and the fold, wall, or overlap that led to it. Ask the reviewer to flag any missing or ambiguous evidence.
3. Compare the submitted answers to the reference. Record any disagreement and its cause. If the drawing changes, regenerate and score that revision.

## Fold and photograph

Print on A4 at **Actual Size (100%)**. Check that the scale bar measures 50 mm between the end ticks. Cut the solid perimeter, crease the dashed lines, tape the seam tab behind the leftmost wall on the net, and tape the base edges. Keep the printed side facing out and the top flaps free.

1. **Signature pair.** Close B then A and take an overhead photo. Reopen both lids, close A then B, and photograph it from the same position. Keep the first flap flat and leave the barcode clear of fingers or tape.
2. **Partial occlusion.** Print the seeded visibility carton. Repeat both orders and photograph the portion of the barcode that remains exposed.
3. **Repair.** Print the seeded repair carton. Put a removable 22 × 6 mm barcode slip inside the marked rectangle on the first lid and photograph the closed result. Move the same slip to the valid rectangle and photograph it again using the same closure order. Check the crease-crossing rectangle on the open net.
